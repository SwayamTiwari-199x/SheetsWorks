## BANNED PHRASES — SOURCE

**The 23 banned phrases in rules.json came from Stage 0 research (RULES.md), NOT from the sheetsresume.com guide.** The guide mentions avoiding "fluff" and "weak words" generally but does not publish a specific banned-phrase list. The current list of 23 phrases (team player, hard worker, detail-oriented, results-driven, results-oriented, self-starter, go-getter, passionate about, think outside the box, outside-the-box, rockstar, ninja, guru, synergy, responsible for, worked on various, involved in, duties included, assisted in, helped with, participated in, excellent communication skills) was compiled from ATS/recruiter best-practice research in Stage 0.

## SECTIONS

| Rule | Source URL | Verified |
|------|------------|----------|
| **No summary section (Sheets template).** Lead with most impressive thing (usually current job). Summaries are weak openers. Blank template keeps summary. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/should-a-resume-have-a-summary-section | unverified |
| **Interests section (one line at end).** Humanizes you, gives interviewer conversation starter. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/why-do-you-recommend-putting-interests-on-a-resume (404; referenced in other pages) | unverified |
| **No references on resume.** Not even "References available upon request." Provide separately if explicitly requested. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/should-i-have-references-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **No photo on resume (US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/IE).** Anti-discrimination laws, subconscious bias, weirdness factor. Photo OFF by default. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/should-i-put-a-picture-of-myself-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **Portfolio link in contact links.** Put in header alongside contact info. Hyperlink descriptive text ("Portfolio", "GitHub"). Not LinkedIn. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/where-to-link-your-portfolio-on-your-resume | unverified |
| **LinkedIn URL: omit by default.** Opens to visual bias/discrimination. Exception: executive-level with strong profile. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/should-i-put-my-linkedin-url-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **Work gaps:** Add "Independent Consultant" role with dates, one bullet: "Needed scheduling flexibility to take care of personal matters, so I worked as an independent consultant for a year." Or last bullet of previous job: "Needed scheduling flexibility to take care of personal family matters, which is why I left this role." | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/how-to-explain-a-work-gap-on-a-resume | unverified |
| **Multiple roles at same company:** Bold overall date range; italic per-role date range next to title. List roles separately to show upward trajectory. Can fold very short early promotions into next title. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/how-do-i-show-multiple-roles-at-the-same-company-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **High school:** Only if in HS/early college with significant extracurriculars. Post-college: 100% omit. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/should-i-put-my-high-school-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **Location in header:** City, State (or nearest big city). For international: add country and sponsorship needs. No street address. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/what-should-i-put-for-my-location-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **Self-employment:** Include if impressive/relevant. Top billing in Work Experience if main income. Otherwise separate section. Ask: "Would I want to talk about this in an interview?" | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/how-do-i-include-self-employment-on-my-resume | unverified |
| **Unfinished degree:** List major, relevant coursework, years attended, GPA/honors. Don't claim degree. No high school unless graduating HS. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/what-if-i-didnt-finish-my-degree | unverified |
| **What belongs on a resume:** Name, professional email, phone, location (city/state), portfolio/GitHub/links, professional experience (company, title, dates, bullets), relevant links. No full address. | https://sheetsresume.com/advice/what-should-be-on-a-resume | unverified |

## FORMATTING

| Rule | Source URL | Verified |
|------|------------|----------|
