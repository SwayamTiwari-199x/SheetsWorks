Welcome to Sheetsworks

A resume builder that shows you the real exported PDF while you write, and tells you where you are breaking the rules. Two formats; see RULES.md for where each one comes from.

This Week

Latest Release

Two resume formats. blank prints every section including a summary. sheets drops the summary and finishes with a one-line Interests entry. Switching never deletes anything you have written.

They are kept apart because the two sources genuinely disagree about summaries. RULES.md §3a records both rather than picking for you.

Formats

blank

Every section, including a summary. The researched default, and the one our own sources put Summary in.

sheets

No summary. Finishes with a one-line Interests entry. One page whatever your experience, and files are named “First Last Resume”.

AI Resume Builder

Not built yet.

This is where the assistant belongs: suggesting sharper phrasing for each bullet, rewriting a weak line, and telling you which of your bullets are too long to be read.

Why it is empty rather than half-working

The provider this project is configured against currently rejects every available model for this account, so anything built now would fail silently or lie about what it did. An assistant that guesses is worse than no assistant on a document you are about to send.

Until then the parts of this that need no language model still work: the layout obeys the researched section order, the export scales margins and type to stay inside the page budget, and the bar above the preview tells you where you stand.

What works instead, right now

  • hint — the single most useful next thing.
  • ls and cat — see what is in each section.
  • grep — search every word you have written at once.
  • demo — a finished resume to compare against.
  • The preview on the right is the real exported PDF, not a mock-up.
